## Maker 的第一条评论 · First comment

开头那段「你定」的对话是写法，不是转述；不像你们的话，换成你们的那句。

### 中文

```
大家好，我是坤怿，在加拿大。

这个东西最早不是产品，是我做给女朋友的。每天都有这一轮：「今天干嘛？」「随便，你定。」我想有个东西替我先定出来。

它就是一个网页。点一下，它拿现在的时间、预算、天气、想跑多远，把今天排成一条时间轴——附近真实的店，来自 OpenStreetMap，每一站底下一句手写的话（陶艺课那张写的是「做丑了统一称为抽象派」）。然后整份计划封进一个链接。

我最在意的是她那一面。她拆开一枚封蜡，先看到那句话，再看到安排。点「我答应你 ♥」，复制一句回话发回来；下一个按钮是「下次换你排 →」，名字已经对调好了。这就是全部：被安排的人，变成下一个安排的人。

两件别处没见过的事：我用中文排，她可以用英文看，同一份计划、她的语言。以及没有任何东西要注册，计划就在链接里，不在谁的账号里。

不用登录，不是 App，没有付费项。想知道你们发出去之后发生了什么。
```

### English

```
Hi PH — Kun here, from Canada.

This didn't start as a product. It started as a fix for one conversation my girlfriend and I kept having: "What do you want to do today?" "I don't know, you pick." So I picked. Then I wrote the picking down as a website.

It's one page. You tap once, and it takes the time right now, your budget, the weather and how far you feel like going, and lays today out as a timeline — real places nearby from OpenStreetMap, with a hand-written line under each stop (the pottery class says "Anything ugly is officially abstract"). Then the whole plan gets sealed into a link.

Her side is the part I care about. She opens a wax seal. She reads the note before she sees any plan. She taps "I'm in ♥", copies a reply line back to you, and the next button is "Your turn next time →" with the names already swapped. That's the whole loop: whoever gets planned for becomes the next planner.

Two things I haven't seen elsewhere: I write the plan in Chinese and she reads it in English — same plan, her language. And there is nothing to sign up for. The plan lives in the link itself, not in anyone's account.

No login, no app, nothing to pay. I'd love to hear what happens when you send one.
```
