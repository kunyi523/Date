About

Gus, then Lou

Gus & Son has been a neighbourhood barbershop on King Street East since about 1956. Agostino “Gus” Di Nardo opened it. He died in 2020. His son Louie (Lou) Di Nardo still runs the chair.

It is a traditional walk-in shop: espresso while you wait, an old-school feel, soccer on the big screen. Regulars mention about $20 out the door — cheaper than the chain haircut stores, as they put it.
